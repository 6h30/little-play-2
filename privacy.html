Little Play 2: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Little Play 2 is a set of STEAM learning games (science, technology, engineering, art and math) for children aged 3 to 5. It is made to be safe for young children and for the grown-ups who give them a phone or tablet.

The short version

Little Play 2 does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

The app remembers a few settings (volume, voice on or off, your child's age group, the play timer and the stars each game has earned) in the device's own storage so they are still there next time. This information never leaves the device and we cannot see it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Sound and voice

The games make sound with the device's speaker. Spoken words use the text-to-speech voice already installed on the device. The app does not use the microphone or the camera and does not record anything.

Internet and links

The Android app works completely offline. It does not send information anywhere. The web version at 6h30.github.io/little-play-2 is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The app has no links that take a child out of the app. The parent area is protected by a grown-ups check.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect any from children under 13 (or any age). It contains no advertising and no third-party services that could.

Credits

All sounds are made inside the app and the pictures are standard emoji, so the app includes no third-party recordings or artwork.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: please open an issue at github.com/6h30/little-play-2/issues.
