Your child can't leave the app by accident. To unlock, hold the 🔒 button for 3 seconds. The first time, Android asks you to confirm.

Keep your child in the app: turn on Guided Access once in Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access. Then triple-click the side button (or the Home button) while the app is open and tap Start. Your child can't leave the app until you triple-click again.

How the games help your child learn
  • Science: predict, then find out. Before a ball is dropped in the water, the child guesses "sink or float?". Children this age learn most when they make a guess and then see what really happens.
  • Technology: first steps in coding. Putting arrows in order to guide a robot teaches sequences and "what comes next", the idea behind every program. Pixel pictures show how a screen is made of tiny squares.
  • Engineering: build, test, improve. Stacking blocks, fitting gears and rolling a ball down a ramp all let the child try something, watch it work (or wobble) and try again.
  • Art: colours, patterns and symmetry. Mixing paint, continuing a pattern and painting in a mirror build the eye for how things repeat and fit together.
  • Math: count, add, compare. Counting real things, adding with pictures, balancing a scale and putting things in size order come before written numbers.
  • No losing, no clock, no ads. A wrong answer wiggles and the child tries again, with a hint after a few tries. The games get a little harder after two right answers in a row and a little easier after two misses, so they stay fun and not frustrating. The stars on each game only go up.
  • Short sessions, play together. The WHO advises no more than 1 hour of screen time a day at ages 2 to 4, and less is better. The American Academy of Pediatrics suggests playing along with your child. That is why there is a timer and a "see you next time" screen.
  • Tip: sit next to your child, say the words out loud together and ask "What do you think will happen?" before each try.

iPhone/iPad tip: turn the ringer on (the side switch) to hear sound. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen", to open it like an app. It works without internet after the first visit.
